From: Stephen Cheung <scheung@harmony.com>

Subject: Clean-up Campaign

Dear Emma,

I am going to organise a clean-up campaign to improve the local environment. The Manager has asked me to suggest the target participants and a promotional method for the event. I remember you organised a park clean-up activity successfully last year. Can you give me some ideas?

I also need to invite speakers for a recycling workshop. Could you tell me what to do? Thanks a lot.

Regards,
Stephen

Assessment Language Focus

Master these core structures assessed in the Vocational IA Examination.

1. Making Suggestions & Providing Reasons

  • Structure A (Base Verb): It might be a good idea to + [base verb]

    e.g., It might be a good idea to invite employees and local residents.

  • Structure B (Gerund -ing): I suggest + [verb-ing]

    e.g., I suggest using social media platforms.

  • Linking Reasons: because / as / since

    e.g., ...because they can reach a wide audience quickly.

2. Instructions, Modals & Conditionals

  • Sequential Connectors:
    First → Then → Next → After that → Finally
  • Modal Imperatives: should + [base verb]

    e.g., You should identify suitable speakers at least 1 month before.

  • Conditional Clauses: Main Clause + IF + [present simple]

    e.g., You should arrange parking if you invite external speakers.

Highlighted Model Response

Suggestions Sequence Words Modals / Imperatives If-Conditionals Connectives

Dear Stephen,

Thank you for your email about the Clean-up Campaign. I am writing to make suggestions and tell you how to invite speakers for the recycling workshop.

Regarding the target participants, it might be a good idea to invite employees and local residents because they are likely to make a positive impact on the community. Also, they can adopt sustainable habits in their daily lives. As for the promotional method, I suggest using social media platforms because they can reach a wide audience quickly and interactively. In addition, the cost of promoting online is low.

To invite speakers for the recycling workshop, first, you should identify suitable speakers at least 1 month before the workshop. Then, you should prepare formal invitations to the speakers including event details such as event objectives and the schedule. Next, you should submit the proposed workshop arrangements for the Manager’s approval. After that, the Event Coordinator should contact the speakers. Finally, you should arrange transportation and parking if you invite external speakers. You should inform related departments if you invite internal speakers.

Please let me know if you need more information.

Regards,
Emma
